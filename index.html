Hi, I'm Sophia

I'm a freelance features writer specialising in technology and business strategy — specifically how innovation, technology, and business collide to save lives, save the planet, and sell stuff.

I'm drawn to ideas that challenge convention or apply academic theory in unexpected ways. I write long-form features, reported essays, and branded content that earns its audience's attention.

My work has appeared in Wired UK, BBC Worklife, GQ, Contagious, and Weapons of Reason. I also work with brands and agencies on trend reports, sponsored content, and creative strategy.
